1. About Hox AI Tutor

Hox AI Tutor is an AI-powered educational application designed for Indian school students, primarily those studying the CBSE curriculum. The App offers an AI tutoring chatbot, curriculum-aligned learning content, quizzes, progress tracking, and gamification features such as XP points, streaks, badges, and a leaderboard.

The App is developed and operated by Hox Infotech Private Limited, a company registered in India.

2. Who This Policy Applies To

This policy applies to all users of the Hox AI Tutor App, including students, parents, and guardians. Because our primary user base consists of school students (including those under 18 years of age), we treat the App as directed at children under Indian law and apply enhanced privacy protections accordingly.

5. AI-Powered Features

The App includes an AI Tutor chatbot. When a student asks a question:

  • The question text, the subject, and the student's class/grade level are sent to our backend server at aiai.hoxinfotech.com.
  • Our backend uses retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to answer from curriculum-aligned content, powered by OpenAI's GPT-4o model.
  • The student's name, email, phone number, and authentication token are not sent with the AI request.
  • AI conversation histories are stored on our servers for up to 1 year to enable contextual follow-up responses.
Important: AI-generated answers are for educational guidance only and may contain inaccuracies. They should not be treated as a substitute for a qualified teacher's instruction.

5A. OpenAI Data Processing

Our backend sends only the question text, subject, and class/grade level to OpenAI's API. OpenAI processes this data to generate a response. Under our API agreement with OpenAI, data sent through the API is not used to train OpenAI's models. OpenAI may retain API inputs and outputs for up to 30 days for abuse and misuse monitoring, after which it is deleted. OpenAI processes data in the United States.

For more information, see OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

6. Voice Input

The App supports voice input for asking questions to the AI Tutor. When you use voice input:

  • Your voice is converted to text entirely on your device using the Android platform's built-in speech recognition service.
  • No audio recordings are sent to our servers or to any third-party cloud service by the App.
  • Only the resulting text transcription is sent to our backend as a regular text question.
  • The App requests the RECORD_AUDIO permission solely for this on-device speech-to-text feature.

Note: The on-device speech recognition service is provided by your device manufacturer (typically Google). Its own privacy terms apply to the on-device processing. The App itself does not transmit, store, or process any audio data.

8. Data Storage & Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the personal data we handle:

  • Encryption in transit: All data transmitted between the App and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Encryption at rest: Our PostgreSQL database and AWS S3 storage are encrypted at rest.
  • Password security: User passwords are hashed using bcrypt before storage. We never store plain-text passwords.
  • Authentication: The App uses JSON Web Tokens (JWT) for secure session management. Authentication is handled entirely by our own servers — we do not use third-party authentication providers.
  • Sensitive data on device: Authentication tokens are stored in encrypted on-device storage (Flutter Secure Storage).
  • Server logging: Our backend server logs do not record student IP addresses. Server logs are retained for 24 hours only and are then deleted.
  • Infrastructure region: All backend infrastructure (servers, database, file storage) is hosted in AWS Asia Pacific. Firebase services operate in Google Cloud Asia Pacific.

10. Children's Privacy

Hox AI Tutor is designed for school students, including those under the age of 18. We recognise the importance of protecting children's personal data and have built the App with the following safeguards:

  • No advertising: The App does not display any advertisements.
  • No behavioural tracking: We do not track, profile, or monitor students' behaviour for any purpose beyond delivering the educational service.
  • No targeted content: We do not use personal data for targeted marketing or personalised advertising.
  • No device identifiers: The App does not access or transmit Android Advertising ID (AAID), IMEI, MAC address, or other device identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics or social SDKs: The App does not include any analytics, crash reporting, social media, or advertising software development kits.
  • Minimal data collection: We collect only the data necessary to provide the educational service.
  • Anonymous leaderboard: The leaderboard displays ranks and scores only — students' names are not visible to other students.

10A. India — Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025, a "child" is any person under 18 years of age. As our App is used by school students who may be under 18, we are committed to complying with the DPDP Act's requirements as they come into effect, including provisions relating to verifiable parental consent. We will update this Privacy Policy and implement any required consent mechanisms in accordance with the timelines set by the Indian government.

10B. Google Play Families Policy

Where applicable, we comply with Google Play's Families Policy requirements, including restrictions on data collection, SDK usage, and content standards for apps directed at children.

12. Account Deletion

You can delete your Hox AI Tutor account and all associated data through either of the following methods:

  • In-app: Navigate to your account settings within the App and select the account deletion option.
  • Web: Visit https://sites.google.com/view/hoxaitutor-privacy/home and follow the instructions to request account deletion.

What happens when you delete your account:

  • All your personal data is permanently deleted, including your profile information, quiz history, AI conversation history, learning progress, XP, badges, and streaks.
  • Your FCM registration token is disassociated from your account.
  • Deletion is permanent and irreversible.
  • Deletion is completed within a reasonable timeframe after your request.

13. Cookies & Local Storage

The App is a native mobile application and does not use browser cookies. The App stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Authentication token: Stored in encrypted device storage (Flutter Secure Storage) to keep you signed in.
  • App preferences: Stored in shared preferences for a personalised experience.

This local data remains on your device and is not transmitted to any third party. It is removed when you uninstall the App.

14. Push Notifications

The App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to send push notifications about learning reminders and educational updates. To deliver these notifications, a unique FCM registration token is generated on your device and sent to our servers. This token is a random identifier — it does not contain your name, phone number, or any personal information.

You can disable push notifications at any time through your device's system notification settings for the Hox AI Tutor app.
